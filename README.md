# 🏛️ City Policy Red-Teaming & Simulation Engine

An AI-driven stress-testing framework designed to detect loopholes, bypass strategies, and unintended consequences in urban regulations **before** they are enacted.

---

## 🌟 Overview

Drafting effective public policy is difficult—people always find workarounds. This project acts as an automated **adversarial red-team** for urban governance. When a new city rule is submitted, the engine simulates how different demographic groups will attempt to exploit it, calculates the exact mathematical impact (traffic congestion, emission spikes, lost revenue), and issues an automated **Vulnerability** or **Approval** report.

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Adversarial Persona Engine**: Evaluates every rule against 3 distinct real-world perspectives to discover 3–5 common exploitation routes:
   - 🏢 **The Business Owner:** Searches for legal, financial, and tax policy loopholes.
   - 🛠️ **The Daily Worker:** Identifies low-cost, fast workarounds to preserve income.
   - 🏎️ **The Rich Resident:** Attempts to leverage wealth to bypass restrictions and inconvenience.

2. **Quantitative Impact Analysis**: Uses reasoning models and math heuristics to calculate precise fallout metrics (e.g., extra tons of $CO_2$, percentage increase in gridlock).

3. **Automated Reporting**:
   - 🚩 **Vulnerability Report:** Details discovered loopholes, calculated metrics, and mitigation recommendations.
   - ✅ **Approval Report:** Verifies policy resilience and gives a green light for real-world deployment.
